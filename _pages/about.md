I am a **Senior Engineer** and **Ph.D. Candidate** at Xi'an University of Technology, currently working as a **Technical Expert** at Qinxin Intelligent Computing (Shaanxi) Technology Co., Ltd. 

I have over 11 years of experience in mobile communication network management software, intelligent operations and maintenance (AIOps), and artificial intelligence research & development. My research spans algorithm formulation, core module architecture, and large-scale industrial deployment. The LTE-OMC network management system I co-developed has been widely deployed across more than 30 provincial regions in China.

### Research Interests
- **AI Technologies & Industrial Applications**
- **Spatio-Temporal Data Intelligence & Mobile Traffic Prediction**
- **Cross-City Knowledge Transfer & Graph Deep Learning**
- **4G/5G Network Management, AIOps & Intelligent Energy Saving**
